Connect a QuickBooks company

Fluxclaw connections are owner-authorized and read-only. This page does not initiate a connection by itself.

To connect a company, start the local Fluxclaw connector on the authorized Mac, then complete the Intuit approval flow in the same browser session. Review the requested scope before approving it.

Fluxclaw does not create, edit, delete, or transmit QuickBooks records through this connection. You can disconnect at any time.

Need help or want a connection removed? Use the approved support channel for your Fluxclaw account.
