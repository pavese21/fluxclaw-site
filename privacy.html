Privacy Notice

Effective date: Draft for owner review before publication.

Fluxclaw provides digital resources and workflow tools for independent artists. This draft describes the intended handling of information on this site and in approved Fluxclaw services.

Information we receive

We may receive information you choose to provide through a purchase, support request, or approved account connection, including contact details, order information, and records needed to provide the requested service.

How information is used

Information is used to deliver products and services, provide support, maintain security, prevent fraud, and meet legal obligations. Fluxclaw does not sell personal information.

Payments and connected accounts

Payments are handled by the provider used for the transaction. Read-only account connections require explicit authorization and are used only for the requested intelligence or reporting function. Connected financial records are not used to train public AI models.

Retention and security

Information is retained only as long as needed for its purpose, legal obligations, or legitimate security needs. Access is restricted and secrets are stored separately from ordinary application data.

Your choices

You may request access, correction, deletion where applicable, or disconnection of an authorized account connection through the business's published support channel.

Changes

This notice may be updated when services or legal requirements change. The effective date will be updated when a reviewed version is published.
