Disconnect a QuickBooks company

Disconnecting removes Fluxclaw's authorization to retrieve new QuickBooks data. It does not alter records held in QuickBooks.

To disconnect, use the local Fluxclaw connector on the authorized Mac or remove Fluxclaw from the connected-apps area of the QuickBooks company. Existing Fluxclaw intelligence records remain subject to the applicable retention requirements until separately reviewed or deleted.

Need help or want a connection removed? Use the approved support channel for your Fluxclaw account.
